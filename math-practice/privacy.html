Math Practice

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Math Practice is an app by Priyans Brahmbhatt for practising times tables and arithmetic on iPhone and iPad. The main games are free, with an optional, one-time Family pack purchase. There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDK or developer-run app server. Practice records stay on your device; they are not sent to the developer.

What the app collects

We do not collect practice records or personal information through the app. An optional first name or nickname stays on your device. The app does not ask for an email address, location or birth year. Answers, scores and handwriting are not sent to us. Apple handles purchases and ratings separately, as explained below.

What stays on your device

The app can keep profiles for up to six children on one device, each with their own practice history and settings. Adding a second child requires the Family pack. Each profile can have an optional first name or nickname of up to 20 characters. Names are kept in the device's local settings and are not sent to us or written to app logs. Dot can use a name in greetings, results, certificates and reminders. You can change or remove it in Grown-ups → Children or from the journey card.

The app saves recorded answers, including the date, game, question, answer, whether it was right and time taken, as well as best scores. These records support progress, stickers, journey and Tricky Facts. It also keeps settings and other app preferences on the device. Under Apple’s App Privacy definition, information processed only on the device is not “collected”.

The app does not sync practice records between devices. Depending on your settings, an iCloud device backup may include local app data, and restoring that backup may bring it back. Older backups can contain older records.

Grown-ups opens directly without a birth-year check. To erase the current child's answers, best scores and related progress, open Grown-ups → Erase Practice History and confirm Erase. Other children's history remains. This does not remove the child's name or settings, refund a purchase, or delete copies already shared. To remove a child when there is more than one, open Grown-ups → Children, select the child and confirm removal; their local history and settings are erased. The last child cannot be removed this way. To remove the app's local data, use Delete App in your device's storage settings, rather than Offload App. A backup or a shared copy may still contain older information.

Widgets and the Play Daily Ten control

The app shares a small Daily Ten summary with its widget and control through a local shared area on the device. It can include the current child's name, a child identifier, last Daily Ten result and days in a row. The Home Screen widget may show the name when there is more than one child; the Lock Screen widget does not show it. The full practice history remains private to the app.

The Quick Question widget keeps its own current questions, today's tally and most recent answers, and the level and kind of sum you choose for it, on the device. Widget answers are not added to the app's practice history. The summary and widget state files are excluded from device backup. The Play Daily Ten control opens the app; it does not send a request to us.

Family pack and Apple

The Family pack is an optional, one-time purchase, not a subscription. Purchasing or restoring it requires a connection to Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment and keeps the purchase record under Apple's privacy policy. The app receives purchase and entitlement information to determine whether the pack is unlocked, including eligible Family Sharing access. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password. Apple handles purchase approval, including Ask to Buy where it applies. You can use Restore Purchase in Grown-ups.

Age and parental approval for updates

For people updating from an earlier version, where required, the app uses Apple's Declared Age Range and PermissionKit system prompts. The app may receive an age range and its confirmation status, or an approval result, to decide whether a parent needs to approve or an adult needs to acknowledge the update. It keeps local flags for handling that process. It does not send the age range or approval result to the developer.

Siri and App Shortcuts

Optional App Shortcuts can open games through Apple's system surfaces. No request goes to a developer-run service. Siri and Shortcuts are governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

Optional daily reminder

You can choose a daily reminder and its time. The device asks for notification permission and schedules it locally. With one child, the reminder can include their nickname and may appear on the Lock Screen, depending on your settings. With more than one child, it does not include a name. You can play without notifications, turn the reminder off under Grown-ups → Daily reminder, or turn off notifications in device Settings.

Read aloud and handwritten answers

Read aloud uses Apple's speech synthesiser on the device to speak game text. If you choose handwritten answers, Apple's Vision framework recognises the numbers on the device. The drawing is not uploaded or saved as a practice record; the recognised number is recorded as an answer when the game saves it.

Sharing a certificate or worksheet

A certificate can show a game's result and the child's optional nickname. A worksheet contains arithmetic questions and may include an answer key. A Tricky Facts worksheet is based on questions missed on this device, so it can reveal which facts need practice.

These files leave the app when you choose to save, print or share them using Apple's controls. The chosen app, recipient, printer or storage service handles that copy under its own practices. We do not receive a copy. A parent or carer should help a child decide what to share. Erasing practice history cannot remove files already shared.

Photos

If you choose a Photos save option for a certificate, Apple's system controls handle that action and any permission needed. Math Practice does not browse or read your photo library.

App Store ratings and links

The app may show Apple's optional App Store rating prompt. You can dismiss it and keep playing. Apple handles ratings and reviews; please do not include a child's personal information in a public review. Opening the privacy or support link uses your browser, where the website's hosting and browser services may process normal web requests. Those requests do not contain your practice history.

Children

Math Practice is intended for children aged about 6–10, with adults handling purchases and settings. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app or use it for advertising or tracking. The US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) concerns children's personal information collected online; our practice records and handwriting processing remain local.

The Australian Privacy Act 1988 may apply to a business depending on its circumstances, including its size and activities. We do not claim an exemption or certification under that Act or COPPA. This policy describes how this app handles information. Parents and carers can erase local practice history as described above and contact us with questions.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
