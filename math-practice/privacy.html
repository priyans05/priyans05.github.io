Math Practice

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Math Practice is a free app by Priyans Brahmbhatt for practising times tables and arithmetic on iPhone and iPad. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code, and the app never connects to the internet.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app doesn't ask for a name, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

What stays on your device

Answers, scores and times are kept only while the app is open. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing a certificate

At the end of a game you can make a certificate showing your result and time. It leaves the app only if you choose to share or save it with Apple's share sheet. What is shared is a picture of the certificate and a short message with two links: one to this website and one to the app on the App Store.

Photos

If you choose to save a certificate to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add it. The app only adds the picture; it can't see or read your photos.

Children

Math Practice is made for children to use. Because it collects no information at all, there is nothing to review or delete. A parent or carer may want to be nearby when a child shares a certificate. Parents with any questions can contact the developer at the address below.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
