Math Practice

Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Math Practice is an app by Priyans Brahmbhatt for practising times tables and arithmetic on iPhone and iPad. The main games are free, with an optional, one-time Family pack purchase. There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDK. Practice records stay in the app on your device; they are not sent to the developer.

What the app collects

We do not collect practice records or personal information through the app. It offers an optional first name or nickname that stays on your device. It does not ask for an email address or location. The grown-ups gate asks for a birth year in memory only; it is not saved. It does not send answers, scores or handwriting to us. Apple handles App Store purchases and ratings separately, as explained below.

What stays on your device

The app saves each recorded answer: the date, game, arithmetic question, answer given, whether it was right, and the seconds taken. It also saves best scores, their dates and times. These records make the best scores, progress report, days-in-a-row, stickers and Tricky Facts practice possible. There are no separate child profiles; people using the same app on one device share its history, even if the nickname changes.

You can optionally tell Dot a first name or nickname in Welcome. It is limited to 20 characters and saved in UserDefaults on this device only, never sent to us or written to app logs. Dot uses it in greetings, results, certificates and local reminders. Skip it or leave it blank to play without a name. To edit or remove it, tap the journey card then What should Dot call you?, or open Grown-ups → Nickname. Choose Remove nickname or clear the field and save. Under Apple’s App Privacy definition, information processed only on the device is not “collected”.

The app also remembers settings such as sounds, answer mode and reminder time, which free game trials have been used, which sticker celebrations have been shown, and when it has asked for a rating. These are stored on the device too.

Practice history is kept until you erase it or delete the app. We have no server copy and the app does not sync practice records between devices. Your device's own backup and restore services may include app data, depending on your settings; those services are separate from the app.

To erase saved answers and best scores, open Math Practice, tap Grown-ups, complete the grown-ups check, tap Erase Practice History, then Erase. This also removes progress derived from those records. It does not remove the nickname, reset settings or used free trials, turn off a reminder, refund a purchase, or delete copies of certificates and worksheets you have already saved or shared. To remove all the app's local data, use Delete App in your device's storage settings, rather than Offload App. An older device backup may contain older records.

Family pack and Apple

The Family pack is an optional, one-time purchase, not a subscription. Purchasing and restoring it require a connection to Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment and keeps the purchase record under Apple's privacy policy. The app receives purchase and entitlement information from Apple to check whether the Family pack is unlocked, including eligible Family Sharing access. We do not receive your payment card details, Apple Account password or a child's practice history. The app does not send purchase information to a developer-run server.

Purchases, the reminder settings, and the privacy and support links are behind a grown-ups check. This is a step to involve an adult; it is not identity verification or proof of a person's age. Apple's purchase approval and Ask to Buy controls are separate.

Age and parental approval for updates

For people updating from an earlier version, where required, the app uses Apple's Declared Age Range and PermissionKit system prompts for an age range, adult acknowledgment or parental approval of the significant update. These are Apple system services, separate from the local grown-ups gate. The app does not send an age range, birth year or approval record to the developer.

Siri and App Shortcuts

Optional App Shortcuts let you start Daily Ten, Tables Check, a times table or Math Game through Apple's system surfaces. They open the game in the app; no request goes to a developer-run service. Siri and Shortcuts are governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

Optional daily reminder

A grown-up can choose a daily reminder and its time. The device asks for notification permission, and schedules the reminder locally. The reminder includes the optional nickname and may appear on the Lock Screen, depending on your notification settings. Editing or removing the nickname updates the scheduled reminder and removes the delivered reminder from Notification Centre. There is no push-notification server, contact list or advertising message. You can keep playing without allowing notifications. Turn it off under Grown-ups → Daily reminder, or turn off notifications for Math Practice in the device's Settings.

Handwritten answers

If you choose handwritten answers, Apple's Vision framework reads the numbers you draw with a finger or Apple Pencil on the device. The app does not upload or save the drawing as a practice record. The recognised number becomes the answer and is saved in the same way as a typed answer.

Sharing a certificate or worksheet

A certificate is a picture showing the game's name, result, time, stars and date. It includes the optional nickname if one is saved. Remove the nickname before making a certificate if you do not want it on a shared copy. Sharing it also offers a short message with a link to Math Practice on the App Store.

A worksheet is a PDF containing arithmetic questions and, if selected, an answer key. A Tricky Facts worksheet is based on questions missed on this device, so it may reveal which facts need practice. It does not include the saved answer log, a child's name or their previous individual answers and timings. It has blank spaces for a name, date and score to be written on paper, and Math Practice branding.

These files leave the app only when you choose to save, print or share them using Apple's share or print controls. The chosen app, recipient, printer or storage service then handles that copy under its own practices. We do not receive a copy. A parent or carer should help a child decide what to share. Erasing practice history cannot remove files already shared.

Photos

If you choose a Photos save option for a certificate, Apple's system controls handle that action and any permission needed. Math Practice does not browse or read your photo library.

App Store ratings and links

The app may show Apple's optional App Store rating prompt. You can dismiss it and keep playing. Apple handles ratings and reviews; do not include a child's personal information in a public review. Opening the privacy or support link uses your browser, where the website's hosting and browser services may process normal web requests. Those requests do not contain your practice history.

Children

Math Practice is intended for children aged about 6–10, with adults handling purchases and settings. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app or use it for advertising or tracking. The US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) concerns children's personal information collected online; our practice records and handwriting processing remain local. A grown-ups check is not verifiable parental consent to collect personal information.

The Australian Privacy Act 1988 may apply to a business depending on its circumstances, including its size and activities. We do not claim an exemption or certification under that Act or COPPA. This policy describes how this app actually handles information. Parents and carers can erase local practice history as described above and contact us with questions.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
