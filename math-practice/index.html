Math Practice

Support

Math Practice is an arithmetic and times-tables app for children, on iPhone and iPad. The free games include Daily Ten, Math Game, Times Tables, Tables Check, Climb and Skills, with stickers to collect and “Show me” explanations when an answer is wrong. Children tap an answer or type it. An optional, one-time Family pack adds more games and levels, handwritten answers, a progress report for grown-ups, printable worksheets, and more colours and sounds. There is no subscription and no sign-in.

Questions

Do I need an account or the internet?

No account is needed. The games, and reading handwritten answers, work on your device without an internet connection. Buying or restoring the Family pack goes through Apple's App Store and needs a connection. For some updates, Apple's own age and parental approval prompts may appear. Ratings, links to this website and anything you choose to share use Apple's system apps and services. Nothing is sent to us.

Are answers saved?

Yes, on this device only. Answers and best scores are kept so the app can show progress, award stickers and practise tricky facts. They aren't sent to us, and the app doesn't sync them between devices. Family Sharing shares the purchase, not practice records. Each child's profile on a device has its own history; adding a second child needs the Family pack. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What does Dot call my child?

When the app first opens, Dot asks for a first name or nickname. It's optional, up to 20 characters, and kept on this device only. It can appear on certificates and in reminders, including on the Lock Screen. To change or remove it, tap the level card on the home screen, then Dot calls you … (or What should Dot call you? if no name is saved), or open Grown-ups › Children.

Is there a grown-ups check?

From version 2.1, no: Grown-ups opens directly, and the app doesn't ask for a birth year. Purchases are still approved by Apple, including Ask to Buy where it's set up, and erasing history or removing a child asks you to confirm first. Version 2.0 asked a grown-up for the year they were born; that year was never saved.

How do I restore the Family pack?

Open Grown-ups, then tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the Apple Account used for Media & Purchases and connected to the internet. Family Sharing access is managed by Apple.

How do I erase practice history?

Open Grown-ups, then tap Erase Practice History, then Erase. This removes the current child's saved answers, best scores and the progress worked out from them; other children's history stays. It doesn't remove the child's name (do that under Children), reset settings, turn off reminders, refund a purchase or remove certificates and worksheets already shared. To remove all of the app's data, delete the app (Delete App, not Offload App). Older device backups may still hold older data.

Can I turn off reminders?

Yes. The daily reminder is optional and set up on the device itself. Turn it off in Grown-ups › Daily reminder, or in Settings › Notifications › Math Practice. Children can play without allowing notifications.

Can Siri start a game?

Yes. Ask Siri, or use the Shortcuts app, to start Daily Ten, Tables Check, a times table or Math Game. The game opens in the app and starts with its countdown.

How do I share a certificate or worksheet?

Certificates and worksheets leave the app only when you choose to save, print or share them with Apple's share or print controls. A certificate shows the optional nickname if one is saved. Please help children decide what to share.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Math Practice works on both iPhone and iPad.
