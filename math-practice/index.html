Math Practice is a free arithmetic practice app for iPhone and iPad. Math Table practises times tables from ×1 to ×10 in order: pick a group (1–5, 6–10, 11–15 or 16–20), Random, or Choose any table from 1 to 100, then Play Next to keep going. Math Game asks 20 questions with +, −, × or ÷, at Beginner or Expert (find the missing number) and Easy, Medium or Hard, each with four answer choices. Every game starts with a 3-2-1 countdown and has a timer, and at the end you can review every answer and make a certificate to share.

Questions

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. There is no sign-in, and the app works without an internet connection.

Are my answers saved?

No. Answers, scores and times are kept only while you play. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent to us.

How do I share a certificate?

At the end of a game, tap Generate Certificate on the review screen, then tap the share button at the top right. Apple's share sheet opens, and you choose where to send or save it. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Math Practice works on both iPhone and iPad.
