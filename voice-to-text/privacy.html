Privacy Policy for Voice to Text Keyboard

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Overview

Voice to Text Keyboard lets you talk instead of typing. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone. The app does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not receive, collect, store, or sell what you say or type.

Your Voice

The app uses Apple's on-device speech recognition to turn your speech into text. Your audio is not sent to us or to any other server, and it is not saved. When you start dictating, the microphone stays on for up to 5 minutes after your last dictation, so the next dictation can start from the keyboard. Audio between dictations is not turned into text and is thrown away. You can turn the microphone off at any time in the app, and iOS shows the orange microphone dot whenever it is on.

The first time you use dictation, iOS may download Apple's speech model for your language. That download comes from Apple; Apple's privacy policy applies to it. Learn more at apple.com/privacy.

The Keyboard and Full Access

The keyboard asks for Full Access only so that it and the app can pass your dictated words to each other on your iPhone. The keyboard has no network code: it does not connect to the internet. When it types your words in, it looks at the text just before the cursor, on your iPhone, only to get spaces and capital letters right; it does not store or send that text. It does not read your clipboard or what you type with other keyboards.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device:

  • The words of your latest dictation, only until they are typed into your text
  • App settings, such as whether setup is finished

The app keeps no history of what you said. You can delete this data by uninstalling the app.

Analytics and Tracking

Voice to Text Keyboard does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us; they do not include what you said.

Check It Yourself

In Settings › Privacy & Security › App Privacy Report, you can see which apps used the microphone and contacted the network.

Children's Privacy

Voice to Text Keyboard does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example when the app adds purchases. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
