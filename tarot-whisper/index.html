Tarot Whisper

Support

Tarot Whisper is a free tarot app for iPhone and iPad for learning the cards, turning one card a day and keeping a private journal. It's for reflection and learning, not prediction.

Contact

Email priyans05@gmail.com. Please include your device, its iOS version and the app version (Settings › About in the app). We usually reply within a few days.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and all 78 cards are free.

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent to us. If you delete the app, your journal is deleted with it.

Why don't I see reversed cards?

Reversed cards are off by default. You can turn on Include reversed cards in the app's Settings. It applies to new cards and readings, never to a card you have already drawn.

Does the app tell the future?

No. Card meanings are traditional interpretations to help you reflect. They are not a substitute for professional medical, financial, legal or mental-health advice.

Where does the card content come from?

Every card was written fresh, in our own words, from published sources.
