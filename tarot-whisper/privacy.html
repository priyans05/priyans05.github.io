Tarot Whisper

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Tarot Whisper doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your daily cards, readings, journal entries and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them, and they are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third parties

The app doesn't use any third-party service that receives your data. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These never contain your journal entries.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes, for example when optional iCloud sync or purchases are added, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
