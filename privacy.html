Privacy Policy for Recipe Genius

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Overview

Recipe Genius is committed to protecting your privacy. Recipes are generated locally on your device using Apple Intelligence. We do not require an account and do not collect personal information. To help us improve the app, we use privacy-friendly, anonymous analytics, as described below.

On-Device Recipe Generation

Recipe generation is performed entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence. Your recipes, the prompts you type, and your saved recipes stay on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party.

Local Storage

The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan, allergens)
  • Cooking skill level preference
  • Saved recipes

This data never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

Analytics

To understand how the app is used and to improve it, Recipe Genius uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. TelemetryDeck collects anonymous usage data — such as which features and screens are used and basic app events — together with a non-identifying, anonymized device identifier. This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is never sold. It does not include your recipes, your prompts, or any personal information.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Recipe Genius Pro, purchases are processed by Apple and managed using RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase information (such as your subscription status and purchase history) solely to manage your subscription. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

Apple Intelligence

Recipe Genius uses Apple's on-device AI models (FoundationModels framework) for recipe generation. Apple's privacy policy applies to the use of these models. Learn more at apple.com/privacy.

Tracking

Recipe Genius does not track you across other apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). No App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

Children's Privacy

Recipe Genius does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
